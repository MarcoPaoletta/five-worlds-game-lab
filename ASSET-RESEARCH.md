## YouTube use and credit

KayKit's creator pages label their free editions **CC0 1.0** and allow commercial use without required credit. [CC0's official deed](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/) permits commercial copying and modification, supporting rendered gameplay, tutorials, and monetized videos.

The selected Quaternius pack pages retain CC0 badges. His [current QAL v1.0](https://quaternius.com/license.html), dated 28 August 2026, expressly permits videos and commercial products without attribution. Use the terms accompanying the actual download. QAL prohibits standalone asset/template redistribution and false authorship; link viewers to creator downloads rather than bundling those models in a tutorial repository.

## Download choices

On KayKit's pages choose **Download Now**, then the free-download option, and use the **Free** file. Extra and Source editions are paid and unnecessary for these prototypes. On Quaternius's pages use each pack's individual Download option; paid all-in-one/source offerings are unnecessary.

## Practical import notes

Prefer glTF where offered for a Godot/Xogot workflow. Other packs list FBX, OBJ, and Blend; use a format supported by the installed editor, or convert to glTF in Blender. Actual imports, model contents, collisions, scale, and animations have not been tested in Xogot. OBJ is suitable for static meshes, not skeletal animations. The concepts identify additional art and gameplay work.
